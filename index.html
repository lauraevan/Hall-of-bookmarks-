Highlight Math

Drag the button into your bookmarks bar. Click it once on a page, then highlight math. A floating answer appears automatically and the ⚙ settings button stays in the bottom-right corner.

More compatible now: straight/curly apostrophes, Unicode minus/multiply/divide signs, “what’s”/“whats”/“how much is”, percentages, roots, absolute value, powers, squared/cubed, factorials, modulo, implicit multiplication, and several natural-language forms. The settings panel controls position, size, delay, precision, question text, and auto-answer.
